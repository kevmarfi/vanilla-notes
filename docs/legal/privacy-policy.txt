VANILLA NOTES FOR WINDOWS
PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Vanilla Notes keeps your notes on your computer. It has no account, no server,
and no analytics. It does not collect, send, sell, or share any information
about you or what you write.


WHAT THE APPLICATION KEEPS, AND WHERE

Everything stays on your computer, in the folders of your Windows account.

- Your notes, folders, tags, attachments, and settings (one database per
  profile)
  Where:      %APPDATA%\Vanilla Notes\profiles\
  Encrypted:  Yes, for password-protected profiles

- The list of profiles and, for password-protected profiles, their keys,
  locked by the password and the recovery code
  Where:      %APPDATA%\Vanilla Notes\profiles.db
  Encrypted:  The keys are locked; profile names are not

- Backups of all of the above
  Where:      %APPDATA%\Vanilla Notes\backups\
  Encrypted:  Same as the profiles they copy

- Window position and size
  Where:      %LOCALAPPDATA%\Vanilla Notes\window.json
  Encrypted:  No

- A log of unexpected errors
  Where:      %LOCALAPPDATA%\Vanilla Notes\logs\
  Encrypted:  No

Your password is not stored anywhere, in any form. It unlocks the profile key
while the profile is open and is then discarded. Without the password or the
recovery code, nobody can read an encrypted profile, including the author of
the application.

Windows Hello, when you turn it on for a profile, is handled by Windows. The
application never sees your face, fingerprint, or PIN.

The error log records the time, type, and location in the code of each
unexpected error. It never contains notes, passwords, or keys, and your Windows
account name is removed from it. It stays on your computer; "Copy diagnostics"
in Settings copies it to the clipboard so you can share it if you choose.


WHAT LEAVES YOUR COMPUTER

The application opens no network connections and listens on no ports.
Information leaves your computer only when you do one of the following:

- Clicking a link in a note opens it in your web browser.
- Sending a recovery code by email opens your email program with the code
  filled in; you decide whether to send it.
- Exporting, backing up to another disk, or printing writes a file where you
  choose. Exports are not encrypted, even from a password-protected profile.


DELETING YOUR INFORMATION

- Deleting a note moves it to the trash; it is permanently deleted after 30
  days, or when you empty the trash.
- Deleting a profile from its settings deletes its database and attachments.
  Earlier backups keep their copy until you delete them in Settings.
- Uninstalling the application leaves your notes in place, so they are not
  lost by accident. To remove everything, delete the folders
  %APPDATA%\Vanilla Notes and %LOCALAPPDATA%\Vanilla Notes.


CHILDREN

The application collects nothing from anyone, children included.


CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

Any update to this policy is published with the application release that
requires it, and the date above is updated.
